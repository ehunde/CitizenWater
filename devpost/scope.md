# CitizenWater

CitizenWater helps a citizen report a water problem and follow it until it is fixed.

## The Unique Kernel
It closes the loop: a citizen can report wasted water to a utility, then update the report to say whether the problem is still open or has been fixed.

## Who It's For
Any responsible citizen with a camera phone who notices a treated-water problem anywhere in their community - at a home, business, school, CBD, or other place - and wants to alert the responsible utility. What they do today instead has not been established.

## The Core Loop
The citizen turns on location access, photographs a water problem, and shares the phone's current location coordinates. AI analyzes the photo and uses those coordinates to find the nearest utility in that area, then drafts a report for the citizen to review and send. If they are offline, the report remains pending and the app reminds them when they can send it. They can return to the report after checking the location and mark it fixed or still open.

## Inspiration & Identity
No design or product inspiration has been shared yet.

## Why This Matters to the Learner
They want CitizenWater to help people save water and contribute to UN Sustainable Development Goal outcomes related to water. They also want to learn the plan-first workflow and get the app working.

## What "Working" Looks Like
A short demo follows one treated-water problem in a community from a phone with location access enabled, through photo capture, current coordinates, AI analysis, and identification of the nearest utility in that area, to a report the citizen reviews and sends and a later update marking it fixed or still open. It also shows a report waiting while offline and a reminder when connection returns. The proof is that the citizen can report and follow up, not that every utility worldwide is connected.

## The POC Boundary
One end-to-end example report for a citizen noticing a treated-water problem somewhere in their community: enable phone location access, capture a photo and current location coordinates, use AI to analyze the photo and identify the nearest utility in that area, produce a draft for the citizen to review, hand it off through one utility contact path, retain it as pending if offline and prompt the citizen when online, then let the citizen update its status after checking the location. The exact sample area, utility, and contact path remain for the next planning step to settle.

## Later
- Worldwide coverage of utility locations and contact details.
- Multiple ways to contact utilities, including calls, email, and social media.
- Broader community participation and follow-up beyond the one demonstrated report.

## Explicitly Cut
- Proving that a utility received, accepted, or fixed the report. CitizenWater can show the citizen's handoff and their own follow-up, but the first proof does not establish a utility-side response.
