The core journey

One report moves from a citizen's phone to a utility handoff and back to a citizen-verified status. Offline reports wait for the citizen's review; reconnecting never sends them automatically.

  1. Start without an account. Home offers Start a report, active reports, and access to the archive.
  2. Enable location, then take a photo. CitizenWater explains why it needs current coordinates before asking for location access.
  3. Analyze and route. AI drafts a concise description; location coordinates find the nearest available utility contact.
  4. Review and send. The citizen checks the photo, description, location, utility, and email destination, then sends.
  5. Follow up. The citizen later marks the issue Still open or Fixed. Fixed reports move to Archived.

Screens and layout

Open each surface to review its behavior and the evidence that will show it works.

01 ú Home No sign-in

Put Start a report first. Show saved active reports with status labels and a clear way to view archived fixed reports. Reports stay available on the same phone.

  • Home opens without account creation.
  • A citizen can begin a report and find saved reports by status.
  • Fixed reports appear in the archive.
02 ú Location and photo

Explain why current location is needed, request access, then provide a prominent phone-photo capture action. Save the current coordinates with the report.

  • The location explanation appears before the permission request.
  • The report advances to utility matching only when coordinates are available.
  • The captured photo is visible in the report flow.
  • If access is off or denied, show why the feature is blocked and a retry path.
03 ú AI analysis and nearest utility

AI analyzes the photo and proposes a short description. CitizenWater uses the coordinates to find the closest available utility in the demo data. If no local match is listed, use the closest available contact even if far away; show its name and distance. If the data has no contact at all, keep the report unsent and explain why.

  • The proposed description is editable.
  • Utility selection is based on the captured location and available utility locations.
  • A far match is visibly identified with its distance before the citizen sends.
  • No utility recipient is invented when the data has no contact.
04 ú Review and email handoff

Show photo, editable description, readable location, selected utility, distance, and email destination together. Let the citizen retake the photo, edit, and choose Send. After email handoff, show Awaiting follow-up; do not imply the utility has read or fixed the problem.

  • The citizen can review the full report and recipient before sending.
  • The report is handed off through email only after the citizen chooses Send.
  • Confirmation shows Awaiting follow-up and distinguishes sending from a utility response.
05 ú Pending report while offline

If internet is unavailable, keep the photo and available report information as Pending on that phone. Resume AI analysis or utility lookup when connected, then prompt the citizen to review and send. Never auto-send on reconnection.

  • A pending report remains visible after closing and reopening the app on the same phone.
  • When connected, the citizen is prompted to continue the report.
  • The citizen must review and explicitly send.
06 ú Report follow-up and archive

Reopen a submitted report to see the details and current status. After checking the location, the citizen marks it Still open or Fixed. For an open issue, they can wait or submit a follow-up report. A fixed report is highlighted and archived.

  • The report details and status are available from Home.
  • The citizen can mark an issue Still open or Fixed.
  • A Still open report can remain active or lead to a follow-up.
  • A Fixed report appears in Archived.

Look and feel

A clear, dependable civic style: calm blues, a light background, strong contrast, readable text, and large controls that are easy to use outdoors. Avoid flashy AI-demo styling.

Decisions and POC boundary

Building now

  • Phone-first PWA report flow with no sign-in gate.
  • Required location, photo capture, AI draft, and nearest available utility match.
  • One email handoff, pending offline reports, and citizen-controlled follow-up.
  • Still open / Fixed statuses, with fixed reports archived.

Deferred

  • Worldwide utility data and multiple contact channels.
  • Calls, social posting, user accounts, and cross-device history.
  • Utility acknowledgements or proof of repair.
  • Apple App Store and Huawei AppGallery listings.

The POC demonstrates one selected community using a limited utility-contact set. Store listings, a global directory, and proof that a utility received or fixed a report are not part of this build.

Decisions for the technical plan

  • Demo area and utility contacts: choose a community and sample contact set to demonstrate nearest-utility selection before build.
  • Email handoff: decide how the citizen's email send is completed and confirmed while avoiding any claim that the utility received it.
  • Reconnect prompt: assess how the PWA can notify a citizen if it is closed; regardless, the pending report must be visible and ready to review when they return.
